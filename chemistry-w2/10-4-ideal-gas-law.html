Gas laws and the ideal gas law PV = nRT

Boyle (n, T fixed): PV = const. Charles (n, P fixed): V/T = const. Avogadro (P, T fixed): V/n = const. Together: PV = nRT with R = 8.314 L·kPa/(mol·K). T always in kelvin.
