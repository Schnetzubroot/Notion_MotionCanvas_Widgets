Hydrogen atom: energy levels and spectral lines

En = −hcRH/n² = −2.18 × 10⁻¹⁸ J / n². A jump from ni to nf changes the energy by ΔE = Ef − Ei. ΔE < 0: a photon is emitted; ΔE > 0: a photon must be absorbed. Its wavelength is λ = hc/|ΔE|.
