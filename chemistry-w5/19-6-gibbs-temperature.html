ΔG = ΔH − TΔS: how temperature decides spontaneity

At constant T and P a process is spontaneous when ΔG < 0. ΔG is a straight line in T: it starts at ΔH (T = 0) and has slope −ΔS. If ΔH and ΔS have the same sign, the line crosses zero at T = ΔH/ΔS and temperature decides.
